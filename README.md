- 💳 **Payments and compliance.** Stripe, PayPal and payroll integrations, ledgers, refunds, invoicing, and KYC/KYB verification at ZenHR and Oyster.
- 👥 **Leadership.** I have led and mentored teams of 10+ engineers at ZenHR, Oyster and Toptal.
